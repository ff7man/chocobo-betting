PSX Chocobo Data Generator

Farm race outcomes with four parallel, headless DuckStation workers. This can collect data much faster than the previous BizHawk + Lua workflow. Export CSVs to extend PSX Computed.

Four isolated emulator workers run classes C, B, A, and S concurrently. The launcher reports throughput and estimated time remaining, and can resume a run after interruption.

Use the data

  • Export six racer records per race in the ml/race.lua CSV format.
  • Generate short races for C and long plus short races for B, A, and S.
  • Merge new rows into PSX Computed and rebuild its compact winner database.
Local requirements: build the patched DuckStation source and supply your own FFVII disc image, BIOS, and class save states. This page and the source repo do not include compiled binaries or generated race data.
